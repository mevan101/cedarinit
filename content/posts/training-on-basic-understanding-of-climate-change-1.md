#### **Overview**

Cedar Initiative successfully organized an online training session titled **“Basic Understanding of Climate Change”** on **5 November 2025** via Google Meet. The session aimed to enhance participants’ foundational understanding of climate science, global and local climate change impacts, and possible adaptation and mitigation strategies.

The training brought together youth volunteers and environmental enthusiasts eager to build knowledge and take action toward sustainable development.

#### **Session Flow**

-   **Host:** *Naimur Rahman Durjoy*, Co-founder, Cedar Initiative  
    Durjoy opened the session by welcoming all participants and briefly introducing the objectives of the training.  
      
    
-   **Facilitators:**
    -   *Fariha S Aumi*, Chair, Brighters  
          
        -   **Topics Covered:** *Weather, Greenhouse Gases (GHG), Climate, Climate Change*
        -   Aumi discussed the fundamental differences between weather and climate, explained the role of greenhouse gases, and illustrated how human activities contribute to global climate change.  
              
            
    -   *Jimran*, Chief Executive Officer, MAGIC Initiative  
          
        -   **Topics Covered:** *Climate Change Impact (Local and Global), Adaptation, Mitigation*
        -   Jimran focused on the global and national impacts of climate change, highlighting real-world examples from Bangladesh. He also explained practical adaptation and mitigation measures to reduce vulnerabilities and enhance community resilience.  
              
            
-   **Vote of Thanks:** *Md. Shahriar Azad Evan*, Founder, Cedar Initiative  
    Evan concluded the session by expressing gratitude to the facilitators and participants for their active engagement and valuable contributions.  
      
    

#### **Participation**

Over **37 participants** joined the session from various institutions and organizations. Participants actively engaged through Q&A, reflections, and discussions, demonstrating strong enthusiasm for environmental learning and youth-led climate action.

A participation attendance sheet was maintained for all attendees.

#### **Key Takeaways**

-   Enhanced understanding of basic climate concepts and their interconnections.
-   Awareness of the local and global impacts of climate change.
-   Practical knowledge of adaptation and mitigation strategies.
-   Strengthened youth network for climate education and action.  
      
    

#### **Conclusion**

The training session successfully met its objective of building fundamental knowledge about climate change among youth participants. Cedar Initiative looks forward to organizing more such sessions to foster climate awareness and inspire collective action toward a sustainable future.
