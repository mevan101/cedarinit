In a significant move toward building a technologically advanced generation, **Cedar Initiative** has successfully organized the **Computer Science Education Week (CS Ed Week) 2025** at the JAAGO Foundation School, Rangpur. The event aimed to bridge the digital divide and spark interest in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and future-tech among underprivileged yet brilliant young minds.

#### **Fueling Curiosity in AI and Robotics**

The students of JAAGO Foundation School Rangpur Branch displayed an extraordinary level of enthusiasm. The workshop moved beyond traditional learning, focusing on:

-   **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** Demystifying how AI works and its role in the modern world.
    
-   **The Future Landscape:** Discussing how technology will reshape careers and daily life by 2030 and beyond.
    
-   **Hands-on Engagement:** Interactive sessions that encouraged students to think critically and solve problems using computational logic.
    

#### **Collaborative Synergy**

This impactful event was made possible through the strategic partnership with **Enhanfy Global Ltd.** and **Youth for NextGen**, whose support was instrumental in bringing these advanced concepts to the grassroots level.

> “The curiosity we witnessed today in the eyes of these students is a testament to the untapped potential of Rangpur. At Cedar Initiative, we believe that this introduction to AI is not just a lesson; it is the beginning of their journey toward leading a Smart Bangladesh.”
> 
> *Representative, Cedar Initiative.*

#### **Our Vision for a Smart Bangladesh**

At **Cedar Initiative**, our mission is to empower the youth with the tools of tomorrow. We believe that by nurturing the curiosity of these young learners today, we are laying the foundation for a sustainable, tech-driven, and “Smart” Bangladesh.

The journey that started today at the Rangpur branch of JAAGO Foundation School will continue to expand, reaching more young minds across the country.
