Md Shahriar Azad Evan, the Founder of **Cedar Initiative**, has been invited to attend the **8th Meeting of the Conference on Interaction and Confidence Building Measures in Asia (CICA) Youth Council**. The prestigious event brings together young leaders from across Asia to foster regional dialogue, peacebuilding, and sustainable cooperation.

As the Founder of Cedar Initiative, Evan has been actively working to empower youth and communities through environmental education, leadership, and sustainable action. His participation in the CICA Youth Council represents a significant step in amplifying the voice of Bangladeshi youth on an international platform.

Speaking about the opportunity, Evan expressed, *“This platform will not only allow me to represent the vision of Cedar Initiative but also highlight the potential of Bangladeshi youth in driving positive change across Asia.”*

The Cedar Initiative team extends heartfelt congratulations to him for this remarkable achievement and wishes him success in representing Bangladesh and the values of youth-led sustainability at the global stage.
