The first day of *“Ushnota Abhijan 2025”* was successfully conducted at **Tejgaon Government High School**, marking a heartfelt beginning to this year’s winter campaign.

A total of **20 students and 5 guardians** actively participated in the campaign by donating winter clothes and spreading awareness about supporting underprivileged communities during the cold season. The event saw remarkable engagement from teachers, volunteers, and youth members, creating a strong sense of compassion and community involvement.

This initiative aims to inspire young people to stand beside those in need and ensure that no one suffers due to the harsh winter. The campaign will continue across multiple schools and communities in the coming weeks.

**Organized by:** Sopnojoy Foundation and Cedar Initiative
