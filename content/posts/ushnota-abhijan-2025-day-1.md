Ushnota Abhijan 2025 started at **Tejgaon Government High School** in Dhaka. On the first day, **20 students and 5 guardians** donated winter clothes, with teachers, volunteers and youth members helping run the collection.

The drive continues at other schools and in local communities over the coming weeks.

**Organised by:** Sopnojoy Foundation and Cedar Initiative
