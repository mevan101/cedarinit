Md Shahriar Azad Evan, co-founder of **Cedar Initiative**, has been invited to the **8th Meeting of the Conference on Interaction and Confidence Building Measures in Asia (CICA) Youth Council**. The council brings together young people from across Asia to discuss regional cooperation and peacebuilding.

At Cedar, Evan leads programs and partnerships, including our environmental education and youth leadership work. He will represent Bangladesh at the meeting.

*“This platform will let me share Cedar Initiative's work and show what young people in Bangladesh are doing to drive change across Asia,”* Evan said.

The Cedar team congratulates him and wishes him well at the meeting.
