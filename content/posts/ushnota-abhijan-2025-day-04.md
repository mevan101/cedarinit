On the fourth day of *“Ushnota Abhijan 2025”*, the campaign reached **Tejgaon Government Girls High School**, where students and teachers actively participated by donating winter clothes for underprivileged communities.

Their heartfelt contributions once again reflected the true spirit of compassion, unity, and community care. Every donated item symbolizes a step toward spreading warmth and smiles among those in need during the approaching winter season.

The continued success of *Ushnota Abhijan 2025* demonstrates how collective efforts can make a meaningful difference and inspire others to take part in acts of kindness.
