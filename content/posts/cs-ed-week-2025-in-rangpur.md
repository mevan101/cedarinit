For Computer Science Education Week 2025, Cedar Initiative ran a session on computer science and artificial intelligence for students at **JAAGO Foundation School, Rangpur**.

#### What the session covered

-   **How AI works**, explained without jargon, and where students already meet it in daily life.
-   **Technology and work**: how jobs and everyday life are likely to change by 2030.
-   **Hands-on problem solving**: short activities where students worked through problems step by step, the way a computer program does.

#### Who made it possible

The session was sponsored by **Enhanfy Global Ltd.**, with **Youth for NextGen** as program partner. Their contributions (৳3,850 in total) paid for food, participation certificates and transport. See the [CSEdWeek 2025 financial report](/financial-report-csedweek-2025/).

#### What's next

We want to run the same session in more schools outside Dhaka. If your school has a computer lab and would like to host one, [get in touch](/partner/).
